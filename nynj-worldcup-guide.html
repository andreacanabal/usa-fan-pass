WHERE TO STAY
Neighborhoods for every budget
🌟 Midtown Manhattan
Best for convenience — walking distance to Times Square, Penn Station (for NJ Transit to MetLife), great nightlife and fan bars. Pricier but unbeatable location. Expect $200–$500/night during the tournament.
🏙️ Jersey City / Hoboken
15–20 min to MetLife on NJ Transit. Much more affordable than Manhattan ($120–$280/night). PATH train to NYC takes ~10 min. Excellent food scene, great Hudson River views.
💰 Newark (Budget Option)
Closest city to MetLife — NJ Transit takes just 15 min to the stadium. Budget hotels from $80–$150/night. Downtown Newark has improved significantly; stick to well-lit main streets at night.
⚠️

Avoid walking unfamiliar streets in Newark alone after 11pm. Stick to Ironbound neighborhood for food — it's vibrant and safe.

🍺 Best Fan Bars in Manhattan
Midtown / Hell's Kitchen — Dozens of soccer bars along 9th Ave (38th–50th St)

Lower East Side / East Village — Younger crowd, late-night spots

Brooklyn (Williamsburg) — More international fans, craft beer culture
🍔 Best Food Near MetLife
At the stadium: New Jersey BBQ, cheesesteak vendors, food trucks outside gates

East Rutherford: Wood-fired restaurants on Route 3 (10 min walk)

Newark's Ironbound district: Incredible Portuguese and Spanish food — perfect post-match (15 min on NJ Transit)

SAFETY GUIDE
Know before you go
✅ SAFE ZONES
  • MetLife Stadium & immediate surroundings (heavy police presence match days)
  • Midtown Manhattan — all hours, well-patrolled
  • Times Square area (daytime & evening)
  • Brooklyn Heights & DUMBO — upscale, safe at night
  • Hoboken & Jersey City waterfront
  • Grand Central Terminal area
  • The High Line, Chelsea Market
⚠️ USE CAUTION
  • NJ Transit trains late night (after 1am) — stay in well-lit cars, near conductor
  • Penn Station area (34th St) — watch for pickpockets in crowds
  • Port Authority Bus Terminal late at night
  • Times Square after 1am (be alert for aggressive vendors)
  • Subway platforms — stand back from edge, keep phone in pocket
  • Secaucus Junction when crowd is sparse
🔴 AVOID ALONE AT NIGHT
  • Certain areas of Newark (north/west) — unfamiliar streets after dark
  • East New York & Brownsville in Brooklyn
  • South Bronx residential areas off main avenues
  • Harrison NJ industrial area at night
  • Port Authority ground floor restrooms (any time)

POLICE PROTOCOL
Know your rights as a visitor

"You have the right to remain silent" — This is the Miranda Warning. For foreign visitors, it means you are NOT required to answer questions beyond confirming your identity. You cannot be punished for staying silent.

5 EXACT PHRASES TO USE
01
"I am invoking my right to remain silent."
Say this calmly — stops questioning legally
02
"Am I free to go?"
If yes, calmly walk away. If no, you're being detained.
03
"I do not consent to a search."
Say this clearly — even if they search anyway, it protects you legally
04
"I want to speak with my consulate."
Under the Vienna Convention, you have this right if arrested
05
"I need an interpreter."
Police must provide one if English is not your language
📱 FILMING THE POLICE IN NEW YORK
✅ It is LEGAL to film police in public spaces in New York. Stand a safe distance away, do not interfere. Keep filming even if an officer says "stop." Do not obstruct officers physically.
⚠️

Never argue aggressively. Comply physically, dispute legally afterward. If you feel your rights were violated, write down the officer's badge number immediately.

UBER / LYFT TIPS
Beat the surge pricing
⚡ SURGE AVOIDANCE STRATEGY
01
Open app 8 minutes BEFORE final whistle
Beat the post-match spike — prices jump 3–5x in the first 20 min after the game
02
Use designated pickup zone, not main entrance
MetLife has specific rideshare zones marked on the official app
03
Wait 45–60 min if you can (grab food nearby)
Surge usually drops significantly after 45 minutes post-game
04
Set a destination before entering the surge zone
Lock in your price estimate before traffic algorithms detect demand
🚕 TAXI SCAM WARNING
🚨

NEVER get into an unlicensed cab outside the stadium or any NYC location. Only use:

✅ Official NYC Yellow Cabs (metered)
✅ Uber / Lyft app (licensed drivers only)
✅ Via app

Unlicensed drivers near major events will overcharge $100–$300 for short rides. They are illegal and uninsured. If someone approaches you offering a ride — say no and keep walking.
