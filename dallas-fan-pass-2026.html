Hydration Protocol
  • 1 litre per hour in direct sunlight
  • Add electrolytes — water alone isn't enough
  • Pedialyte / Liquid IV at Walgreens & CVS near stadium
  • Avoid alcohol in open sun — accelerates dehydration
  • Light-colored clothing, hat is mandatory
  • Apply SPF 50+ sunscreen every 90 minutes
Heat Emergencies — Know the Difference

Heat Exhaustion — Act Fast

  • Heavy sweating, weakness, nausea
  • Cold, pale, clammy skin · weak pulse
  • Move to shade, hydrate, call for help

Heat Stroke — CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY

  • Hot, dry skin (no sweating)
  • Confusion, slurred speech
  • Unconsciousness — life-threatening, do not wait
Cooling Stations Near AT&T Stadium
AT&T Stadium interior is fully air-conditioned. Fan zones near the stadium have cooling areas with misting fans. Globe Life Field (adjacent) also provides shade.

Section 05
POLICE PROTOCOL
Open Carry State — Remain Calm
Texas allows open carry of firearms. You may see civilians legally carrying visible weapons. Do not touch, approach, or react aggressively. This is legal and normal here.
Your Rights as a Foreign Visitor
  • You have the right to remain silent
  • You have the right to contact your embassy/consulate
  • You do not have to answer beyond identifying yourself
  • Ask "Am I free to go?" — if yes, leave calmly
  • Do not resist arrest even if you believe it's unjust
  • Always carry a copy of your passport + visa
5 Key Phrases

"I do not consent to a search."

"I am invoking my right to remain silent."

"I want to speak to my consul."

"Am I being detained or am I free to go?"

"I need a lawyer / attorney."

Section 02
GETTING TO AT&T STADIUM
No Metro to AT&T Stadium
Unlike other World Cup host cities, Dallas has no direct metro link to AT&T Stadium. Plan your transport in advance.
Trinity Railway Express (TRE) Best Option

Runs from Dallas Union Station and Fort Worth T&P Station. Closest stop: CentrePort/DFW Airport — then short rideshare to stadium.

Use the GoPass app for real-time TRE schedules on match days.

~$2.50 per trip
Uber / Lyft Surge Warning

Pro tip: Order your Uber 10 min BEFORE the final whistle — post-match surge can reach 3–5× base fare as 80,000 fans exit simultaneously.

Rideshare pickup zones are ~0.5 mile from stadium. Walk to them — drivers cannot enter the immediate perimeter.

$15–35 typical (surge varies)
DFW Airport → AT&T ~30 min

Direct Uber/Lyft from DFW International Airport. No transfers needed.

$40–60 USD approx.
Love Field (DAL) → AT&T ~25 min

Dallas Love Field (Southwest Airlines hub). Slightly closer to stadium than DFW.

$35–50 USD approx.
Parking at AT&T Stadium Tailgate Culture

Massive parking lots. Arrive 3 hours early — the pregame BBQ tailgate culture in the lots is legendary.

$50–80 per vehicle (match day)
No Unlicensed Rides
Never accept rides from people approaching you near the stadium. Only use official Uber, Lyft, or licensed taxis through official apps.

Heat Emergency Reminder
Heat Stroke = 911, No Exceptions
Hot dry skin + confusion + unconsciousness = Heat Stroke. Fatal without immediate care. Call 911 and move the person to shade/AC.
While Waiting for 911
  • Move person to air conditioning or shade immediately
  • Remove excess clothing
  • Apply cool (not cold) wet cloths to skin
  • Fan the person vigorously
  • Do NOT give fluids if unconscious
  • Stay on the line with the 911 operator
