⚽ FOR EUROPEAN FANS

Boston is incredibly safe by international standards. You will feel very at home here — more so than any other US host city.

👮 POLICE PROTOCOL
  • →Boston Police are generally professional, experienced with tourists and international events
  • →Massachusetts has strong civil liberties protections — know your rights
  • →LEGAL IN MA: Filming police officers in public is fully permitted
  • →Always remain calm and polite — Bostonians respect directness
  • →If stopped: state your nationality clearly, carry your passport copy

📍 KEY FACT

Gillette Stadium is in Foxborough — a small town 30 miles south of Boston. Plan your transport BEFORE match day.

🚉
MBTA Commuter Rail
Providence/Stoughton Line → Foxboro Station
BEST OPTION — 45 min from Boston South Station
⚠ MATCH DAYS ONLY BUY IN ADVANCE
  • →Special match day service only — does not run on normal days
  • →Buy your return ticket before boarding — queues are extreme post-match
  • →Last train back fills immediately — position near stadium exit 20 min before final whistle
  • →Book at mbta.com or the MBTA app
⚡ PRO TIP

Get your return seat position confirmed before kick-off. Missing the last train means a $100+ surge Uber or being stranded in Foxborough.

🚗

🗺 CAN'T-MISS EXPERIENCES
  • ★Freedom Trail — 3km self-guided walk, free, through 400 years of American history
  • ★Fenway Park Tour — Oldest MLB ballpark (1912). Iconic. Book in advance.
  • ★Day Trip: Cape Cod — 1.5 hrs south. Beaches, seafood shacks, quintessentially New England
  • ★Day Trip: Salem — 30 min north. The witch trials city. Fascinating history year-round
  • ★Day Trip: Newport, RI — 1 hr south. Gilded Age mansions, harbor views, Cliff Walk
  • ★North End — Boston's Italian quarter. Best cannoli, espresso, pasta in New England

⏰ LAST CALL — IMPORTANT

Massachusetts last call is 2am — earlier than NYC. Many bars stop serving at 1:45am. Plan your evening accordingly.

👔 DRESS CODE

Boston bars are casual. No dress code issues for football fans. Clean trainers (sneakers) are fine everywhere except upscale hotel bars.
