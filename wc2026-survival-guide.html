Section 2
THE 8 SCAMS

Most common traps near World Cup stadiums. Know them before you see them.

🎯 These scams specifically target international tourists who look unfamiliar with the USA — especially near stadium areas before and after matches.
SCAM 1
The Unlicensed Cab
Outside the stadium, "great flat rate"
▾
How it works
Driver approaches you outside the stadium with a "flat rate" offer that sounds reasonable. No meter, no official markings, no app — just a deal.
🚩 Red flags
No meter visible. Driver approaches YOU (legit drivers don't solicit). Unofficial-looking car. Can't see credentials.
✅ Only use Uber, Lyft, or official yellow/green taxis with meters. Never negotiate with someone who approaches you first.
SCAM 2
Fake Ticket Seller
Counterfeit or already-used tickets
▾
How it works
Someone sells printed tickets outside the venue. They look real. They scan as invalid at the gate — or were already used by someone else.
🚩 Red flags
Selling outside the venue. "Great deal" pricing. Won't let you verify the barcode on your phone before paying.
✅ Only buy from: FIFA official app, Ticketmaster, or authorized resellers. Always scan/verify before handing over money.
SCAM 3
The "Free" CD / Bracelet
Very common: Miami, NYC Times Square
▾
How it works
Stranger puts a CD, flower, or bracelet in your hand — sometimes physically wraps it around your wrist. Then aggressively demands payment. Refusing gets very uncomfortable fast.
🚩 Red flags
Ocean Drive Miami, Times Square NYC, Hollywood Blvd LA are hotspots. Anyone approaching you with an object.
✅ Do not take ANYTHING from strangers on the street. Say "No thank you" firmly without slowing down. Keep walking. Do not engage further.
SCAM 4
Card Skimmer at ATM
Device that steals your card data
▾
How it works
A thin plastic device is placed over the card slot. A hidden camera or overlay captures your PIN. Your card data is cloned and your account drained — sometimes within hours.
🚩 Red flags
Loose or raised card slot. Unusual bulk on the machine. Small camera hole near keypad. ATM in isolated location.
✅ Wiggle the card slot before inserting. Cover keypad when entering PIN. Only use ATMs inside banks or major hotel lobbies.
SCAM 5
The Fake Police Officer
"Drug residue check" on your wallet
▾
How it works
Person in uniform (real-looking or fake) stops you and says they need to check your wallet for "drug residue." Once you hand it over, it's gone — or cash is removed.
🚩 Red flags
Real police NEVER ask to hold your wallet. No marked vehicle nearby. Asks to see your cash specifically.
✅ Ask for badge number. Say you're calling 911 to verify. Real officers will not object. Never hand over your wallet under any circumstances.
SCAM 6
Restaurant Bill Overcharge
Items you didn't order, double gratuity
▾
How it works
Bill arrives with items you didn't order, or with two gratuity lines (one auto-added, one on the signature line to catch the inattentive).
🚩 Red flags
Bill total seems higher than expected. Two lines that look like "gratuity." Mystery charges.
✅ Review every single line before signing. Say: "Could you walk me through this bill? I think there might be an error." Never accusatory — always curious tone.
SCAM 7
WiFi Phishing Hotspot
"Stadium Free WiFi" — it's a trap
▾
How it works
Fake hotspot named something like "Stadium_FreeWifi" or "WorldCup_Guest" intercepts all your data. Passwords, banking, emails — everything goes through the attacker's device.
🚩 Red flags
No password required. Generic name. Connection appears at exactly the right time and place.
✅ Use your eSIM or mobile data. If WiFi is necessary, use a VPN. NEVER access banking or passwords on public WiFi.
SCAM 8
The Bump and Grab
Post-match crowds — most dangerous time
▾
How it works
Person "accidentally" bumps into you hard in a crowd. While you're distracted and apologizing, their accomplice grabs your phone, wallet, or bag.
🚩 Red flags
Crowded areas right after a match. People pushing closer than necessary. Someone in front who seems to be creating a bottleneck.
✅ Keep phone in front pocket or zipped bag. Use a crossbody bag worn in front. Be especially alert the first 30 mins after the final whistle.

Dispute Scripts
🍽️
Restaurant Overcharge
Stay calm. Don't accuse — be curious. The key is to get an itemized review without confrontation.
"Excuse me, I think there may be an error on my bill. Could you go through it with me item by item?"
🚗
Uber / Lyft Overcharge
Do this immediately after your trip — response is faster when you're still close to the ride time.
App → Trip History → Get Help → Fare Review → Submit. Usually resolved within 24 hours.
🏨
Hotel Dispute
Always dispute at check-out, before you leave the property. Much harder to resolve remotely.
"I'd like to speak with the manager about a charge on my bill before I check out. I'd prefer to resolve this now."
💳
Credit Card Fraud
Act immediately. Every hour matters for fraud disputes. Most banks resolve in 3–5 days.
Call the number on the back of your card immediately. Dispute in the app → usually resolved in 3–5 days. Request a new card number sent to your email.
🎫
Fake Ticket — At the Gate
Don't make a scene. Report it, then contact your card for a chargeback.
Tell gate security: "I purchased this ticket and it won't scan. I believe I may have been defrauded. Can you direct me to guest services?" Then file a police report for the chargeback.
📱
Lost / Stolen Phone
Lock remotely first — this protects your data and makes the phone useless to thieves.
iPhone: iCloud.com → Find My → Lock. Android: android.com/find → Secure Device. Then file a police report for insurance purposes.
🛂
Passport / Documents Lost
Contact your country's consulate or embassy immediately — most operate 24/7 emergency lines during major events.
"I am a [nationality] citizen and I need emergency assistance — my passport has been lost/stolen. I am attending the FIFA World Cup and need emergency travel documentation."
