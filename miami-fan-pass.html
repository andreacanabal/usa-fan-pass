⛈️ WEATHER & THUNDERSTORMS
Miami in June: heat + humidity + daily storms — know the pattern
🌩️
DAILY AFTERNOON THUNDERSTORM WINDOW 2pm–6pm most days. Miami has the most lightning strikes per capita in the USA. Take this seriously.
⚡ Lightning Protocol
  • If you HEAR thunder → move to covered structure immediately
  • Hard Rock Stadium HAS a lightning delay protocol — matches can be delayed
  • NEVER shelter under trees or near water
  • Any major hotel lobby or shopping mall is safe
  • Download Weather Channel app — check hourly before outdoor plans
🎒 What to Pack Daily
  • Lightweight rain poncho (not umbrella — wind will flip it)
  • Change of shirt — you WILL get wet
  • Waterproof bag or case for your phone
  • SPF 50+ sunscreen — UV index 10+ regularly. Reapply every 2 hrs
  • Electrolyte drinks — humidity makes heat MORE dangerous than dry heat
🌀 HURRICANE SEASON
June marks the start of Atlantic hurricane season
🌀
JUNE = HURRICANE SEASON START Probability is low but real. Monitor weather.gov or Weather Channel app during your stay.
📋 If a Warning is Issued
  • Monitor: weather.gov or local TV (WSVN Channel 7)
  • Stadium events will be postponed if tropical storm warning issued
  • Shelter: any major concrete hotel, shopping mall (Dolphin Mall, Aventura)
  • Do NOT try to drive away during storm — shelter in place
  • NEVER shelter under trees or near water

🚨 COMMON SCAMS
⛔
THE CD/BRACELET GUYS ON OCEAN DRIVE Men will approach you with "free" CDs or bracelets. Once it's in your hand, they demand money aggressively. Do NOT take anything. Keep walking.
⚠️
DRINK SPIKING — SOUTH BEACH BARS Never leave your drink unattended. Buddy system after midnight. Stick to busy venues.
👮 POLICE PROTOCOL
ℹ️
TWO POLICE JURISDICTIONS Miami-Dade Police covers the county. Miami Beach has its own police. Hard Rock Stadium is Miami Gardens PD. Know who you're talking to.
5 KEY PHRASES
"I don't understand. Do you speak Spanish?"
Miami is 70%+ Spanish-speaking — Spanish works here too
"Am I free to go?"
Establishes if you are being detained
"I am invoking my right to remain silent."
Your right as a visitor in the USA
"I want to contact my consulate."
International law — you have this right if detained
"I do not consent to a search."
You can state this — they may still proceed with probable cause
🏥 HEALTHCARE
Hospitals & Clinics
  • Jackson Memorial Hospital — major trauma center, downtown Miami
  • Baptist Health Urgent Care — multiple Miami locations
  • Heat + humidity = MORE dangerous than dry heat. Hydrate constantly.
🪼 Beach Hazards — Jellyfish
  • Rinse with seawater (NOT fresh water — it worsens stings)
  • Carefully remove visible tentacles with a card or stick
  • Apply heat (hot water pack) to the sting area
  • Seek ER if you have difficulty breathing or swallowing
💳
No Insurance? Say: "I am an international visitor without US insurance. I need emergency care." ERs cannot turn you away.
