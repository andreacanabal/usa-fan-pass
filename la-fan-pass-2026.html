🌡️ Heat Protocol

What you need to know to survive June in LA
☀️ June in LA: 32–38°C, direct sun, low humidity. The open-air seating at SoFi has limited shade. Prepare before you arrive.
💧
Hydration Rules
Follow these or suffer
  • ✅
    Before entering: Drink at least 500ml of water before you pass through the gates.
  • 🧴
    Bring an empty bottle — SoFi has free water refill stations inside. Full bottles may be confiscated.
  • 🧂
    Electrolytes matter: Water alone isn't enough in this heat. Get Liquid I.V., Pedialyte, or similar from CVS/Walgreens.
  • 🍺
    Alcohol accelerates dehydration. Match every beer with a full glass of water.
  • 🕶️
    Gear up: Hat, sunscreen SPF 50+, light-colored clothing. Apply sunscreen before leaving your hotel.
🚨
Heat Exhaustion vs Heat Stroke
Know the difference — it can save a life
⚠ HEAT EXHAUSTION
• Heavy sweating
• Weakness, fatigue
• Cool, pale, clammy skin
• Nausea
• Headache
• → Move to shade, cool water, rest
🆘 HEAT STROKE
• Temp above 40°C
• No sweating
• Confusion / slurred speech
• Flushed, hot skin
• Loss of consciousness
• → CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY

🏥 Healthcare

Hospitals, urgent care & costs
💡 US healthcare is expensive without insurance. Heat-related ER visits can cost $1,500–$5,000+. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
🏨
Nearest Facilities
  • 🚑
    Cedars-Sinai Urgent Care – Multiple locations near West LA/Inglewood. Best for non-life-threatening situations.
  • 🏥
    UCLA Medical Center (ER) – Westwood. Major trauma center, 24/7. ~25 min from SoFi.
  • 🏥
    Centinela Hospital – Inglewood. Closest major ER to SoFi Stadium (~8 min).
💬
No Insurance? Say This
At reception / admissions
"I am an international visitor. I do not have US insurance. I would like a cost estimate before treatment and information about financial assistance programs."
Hospitals are required to treat emergencies regardless. Ask about "charity care" or "financial assistance" programs if cost is a barrier.

Heat: ER vs Urgent Care?
  • 🟡
    Urgent Care: Mild dehydration, headache, fatigue, nausea without confusion. Can administer IV fluids.
  • 🔴
    ER (call 911): Confusion, loss of consciousness, no sweating despite extreme heat, temp ≥40°C, seizure.

👮 Police Protocol

Know your rights in California
🗺️
LAPD vs Sheriff
  • 🔵
    LAPD (Los Angeles Police Dept) – Patrols city of LA: Hollywood, Downtown, Westside, Venice.
  • ⭐
    LA County Sheriff's Dept – Patrols Inglewood, unincorporated areas, and the SoFi Stadium zone during events.
  • 🟢
    FIFA Security – Private event security within the stadium perimeter. Not law enforcement.
⚖️
Your Rights in California
California has stronger protections than most US states
  • ✅
    Remain silent: You have the right to remain silent. Say: "I am exercising my right to remain silent."
  • ✅
    Refuse searches: You can refuse vehicle/bag searches without a warrant. Say: "I do not consent to a search."
  • ✅
    Film police: Legal in California. Stay 8+ feet away and do not interfere. Do not narrate aggressively.
  • ✅
    Ask if you're free to go: "Am I being detained or am I free to go?" – you must receive a clear answer.
  • ✅
    Request a lawyer: If arrested, say: "I want a lawyer." Do not speak until one is present.
🗣️
5 Key Police Phrases
If stopped
"Officer, am I being detained or am I free to go?"
Refusing a search
"I do not consent to a search of my person or belongings."
Staying silent
"I am invoking my right to remain silent."
If arrested
"I want to speak to a lawyer and contact my embassy."
Language barrier
"I need a Spanish/French/Arabic interpreter. I do not speak English well."

~35 min to SoFi

🛡️ Safety Heatmap

Los Angeles by zone
Updated 2026
✅ LA is generally safe in tourist zones during the World Cup with heavy FIFA and LAPD security presence. Use common sense.
🟢 SoFi Stadium Zone / Inglewood (Match Days)
Heavily policed on match days. FIFA security, LAPD, and LA County Sheriff all present. The most secured area in the city during events. Stay near the official perimeter.
🟢 Santa Monica
Very safe day and night. Tourist-friendly, high police visibility near the pier and Third Street Promenade. Normal urban awareness applies after midnight.
🟢 Beverly Hills
Among the safest areas in LA. Private security everywhere. Extremely well-lit commercial zones. Safe day and night.
🟢 Hollywood Walk of Fame (Daytime)
Safe during daylight hours with tourist crowds. Can be gritty late at night — head back before midnight.
🟢 Manhattan Beach / Hermosa Beach
Affluent beach communities. Very safe at all hours. Low crime, family-oriented atmosphere.
🟡 Downtown LA (DTLA) — Late Night
Safe in well-lit areas like the Arts District and Financial District. After midnight, stick to busy streets. Avoid wandering into poorly lit areas. Petty theft is the main risk.
🟡 Hollywood Blvd — After Midnight
Daytime fine, late nights get rough. Panhandling, erratic behavior from homeless population. Don't flash phones or wallets. Leave before 1am if possible.
🔴 Skid Row (Downtown)
Do NOT walk through Skid Row. Large homeless encampment, active drug use, unpredictable. Bounded by 3rd–7th St and Main St–Alameda. Your GPS might route you here — override it.
🔴 South Central / Compton — Alone at Night
As a tourist with no local knowledge, avoid these neighborhoods at night. Some areas are fine in daytime but navigation errors and isolation are risks for visitors unfamiliar with the area.
📱
Universal Safety Tips
  • 🔒
    Keep phone in front pocket or bag zipper – phone snatching is the most common crime against tourists.
  • 🚗
    Lock your car always – smash-and-grab is common. Leave nothing visible: no bags, cameras, or jackets on seats.
  • 💳
    Use RFID-blocking wallet – contactless card skimming exists in crowded areas.
  • 🍺
    Never leave a drink unattended in a bar or club setting.
  • 📍
    Share your location with a friend or family member when going out at night.
  • 🚕
    Only use licensed rideshares – never accept rides from someone approaching you in the parking lot.
🚫
Things NOT To Do
  • ❌
    Do not walk through Skid Row under any circumstances
  • ❌
    Do not accept rides from unlicensed drivers near the stadium
  • ❌
    Do not flash expensive jewelry or cameras in crowded areas
  • ❌
    Do not leave valuables visible in a parked car
  • ❌
    Do not use your phone while walking and distracted at night

🚗 Getting to SoFi

The truth about LA traffic
🚇 THERE IS NO DIRECT METRO TO SOFI STADIUM. The Crenshaw/LAX Line stops at Inglewood — a 20-minute walk or shuttle from the venue. Plan transportation early.
🚦
The Hard Truth About LA Traffic
  • ⏰
    On match days, the entire Inglewood/Century Blvd corridor will be gridlocked for 2–4 hours post-match.
  • 🚗
    LAX to SoFi is normally ~20 minutes. On match day, expect 60+ minutes.
  • 📱
    Uber/Lyft surge pricing can hit 4–6x normal immediately after the final whistle. Open the app 10–15 min before it ends for better rates.
🚌
Option A: Official Metro Shuttle
Recommended — Cheapest & most reliable
  • 🚆
    Take the Metro K Line (Crenshaw/LAX) to Inglewood stations (Downtown Inglewood or Fairview Heights).
  • 🚌
    Board the official FIFA/Metro shuttle from the station to SoFi (approx. 10–15 min, frequent service).
  • 💵
    Metro fare: ~$1.75 with TAP card. Shuttle likely free or included with match ticket — confirm at fifa.com
  • ⚡
    This beats all car options post-match. Metro runs dedicated extra service on match days.
📱
Option B: Uber / Lyft
Convenient but expensive on game day
  • 📍
    Designated rideshare drop-off: Prairie Ave & Century Blvd, north side. Do NOT be dropped in parking lots.
  • 📍
    Designated rideshare pickup: Specific staging zone east of the stadium. Check FIFA/Uber in-app directions on match day.
  • ⏱️
    Book return 10–15 min before final whistle to avoid 4–6x surge. Leave a few minutes early if your team isn't winning.
  • 💵
    Normal fare: $20–40 from West LA. Match day surge: $80–$160+. Budget accordingly.
🅿️
Option C: Driving & Parking
Only if you have no other choice
  • 💵
    Official SoFi parking: $60–$100 per vehicle. Pre-book at sofistadium.com or FIFA site. Sells out.
  • ⏰
    Arrive 2+ hours early. The entire I-405, I-105, and Century Blvd clogs early.
  • 🚶
    If you park in Inglewood side streets (cheaper), expect a 20–40 min walk. Wear comfortable shoes.
  • ⚠️
    Post-match exit takes 60–90 minutes minimum. Pack snacks. Don't leave a full car of people frustrated.
⚠️
Safety Warning: Parking Lot Rides
🚫 DO NOT accept rides from people approaching you in the parking lot offering "easy rides." These are unlicensed operators and represent a safety risk. Only use official Uber/Lyft app or licensed taxis.
